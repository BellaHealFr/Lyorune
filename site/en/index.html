What Lyorune does for you

Easy to set up, light in combat, pleasant to look at. Every element has its own checkbox.

Heal with one click

Every mouse button, alone or with Shift, Ctrl, Alt, casts the spell of your choice on a player's frame. A trinket can go with the spell, and a counter shows which clicks you use.

Hover keys

A keyboard key, an extra mouse button or the wheel casts a spell on the hovered frame.

Everything at a glance

Health, incoming heals, shields, mana, other healers' low mana and their drink, your external spells, heals over time and debuffs visible in the middle of combat, boss auras, big defensives, stunned or feared players, healthy players faded, threat, range, global cooldown and casting. A soft blue edge when your mana runs low, and the five second rule on your cell.

Dispel without searching

The frame lights up when you can remove a magic effect, a curse, a disease or a poison, up to 3 per cell.

Resurrect with the same click

On a dead player, your heal click casts your resurrection spell. Nobody stays on the floor.

Your style

Five themes, high contrast, transparency, size, four cell shapes with icons laid out to fit each one, every icon placed and resized as you like, sorting by group or by role, separate frames for tanks and pets.

Test mode

A fake group of 5 to 40 players to tune everything in peace.

Announcements

Low mana, mana restored, resurrection, with your own wording. The low mana announcement works even when the game hides mana.

Profiles and sharing

One profile per group size, battleground, arena and specialization, applied automatically. Share your setup in game.

Easy to set up

Eleven chapters, a preview cell that shows every setting live, a golden rune on what you changed and a search that finds everything.

Lyorune never changes the game's frames or other addons' frames: it runs alongside ElvUI, Clique and your other interface addons.

Made for World of Warcraft: Forever (WoW Forever), Blizzard's official game: dungeons, 10 to 40-player raids, battlegrounds and arenas. A light alternative to VuhDo, HealBot or Grid for every healer of the MMORPG.

The four paths of healing

Druid, paladin, priest or shaman: Lyorune recognizes your class and adapts to your spells.

Druid

Rejuvenation, Regrowth, Wild Growth: your heals over time show on every frame. Curses and poisons stand out at a glance.

Paladin

Blessings of protection and sacrifice tracked, Cleanse on magic, diseases and poisons. Your Light always arrives in time.

Priest

Renew, Power Word: Shield, Prayer of Healing: shields and heals visible on the frame. Magic and diseases dispelled with one click.

Shaman

Riptide, and Earth Shield on the tank, recalled by the buff panel. Diseases and poisons spotted in an instant.

The runes of Lyorune

At the bottom of the options window, a line of golden runes lights up from time to time.

"Whomsoever takes up this blade shall wield power eternal." The inscription on Frostmourne's dais, in Warcraft III.

Each rune stands for one letter of the sentence, in Elder Futhark. In game, hover the runes: they light up and their translation appears.

Installation

Three steps, and a one-minute tutorial welcomes you in game.

  1. Download the latest version from the Releases page or from CurseForge, the only two official sources.
  2. Unzip it into World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns\. You should get a Lyorune folder.
  3. Start the game. No group at hand? Type /lyorune test to try your clicks on a fake group.
CommandEffect
/lyoruneOpen the options
/lyorune testShow or hide the fake group
/lyorune tutoRestart the tutorial
/lyorune bugReport a problem

Frequently asked questions

What is Lyorune?

Lyorune is a free healing frames addon for World of Warcraft: Forever. It shows your whole group or raid and casts your heals with one click on a player's frame.

Which healing addon should I use on WoW Forever?

Lyorune is made for World of Warcraft: Forever (interface 16001). It is lighter than VuhDo and HealBot, handles values hidden in combat and suits priests, druids, paladins and shamans.

Is Lyorune free?

Yes. Lyorune is free and is downloaded from its GitHub Releases page or from CurseForge, its only two official sources.

Does Lyorune work with ElvUI or Clique?

Yes. Lyorune never changes the game's frames or other addons' frames. It runs alongside ElvUI, Clique and other interface addons.

How do I install Lyorune?

Download the archive from GitHub, unzip it into World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns\ and start the game. A one-minute tutorial opens on first login.
