I have been healing in MMORPGs for years. Raid nights watching forty health bars, dungeons where everything hangs on a heal cast half a second earlier, PvP fights where you have to read the battle before it turns. Lyorune was born from that: the wish for a tool that does not claim to be perfect, but helps every healer keep their companions on their feet, in raids, in dungeons and in PvP alike.

**1.26.0 is the biggest interface update since Lyorune was born.** The options window has been fully redesigned and modernised. The old one stacked tabs; the new one reads like a healer's spellbook:

- **eleven chapters sorted by what you want to do**, in a sidebar, with a Home page that sums up your profile and your choices;
- **a preview cell** that shows every setting live, without closing the window or finding a group;
- **a golden rune** next to every setting you have changed, so you can see at a glance what is yours and go back in one click;
- **a search** that finds a setting by its name or its explanation, with or without accents;
- **the essentials in sight**, the less common settings folded under Fine settings;
- **restful windows** for long evenings: deep grey instead of black, text that does not dazzle, windows that adapt to your screen and no longer cover each other;
- **a touch of magic** to remind you what it is all for: a gold and green healing light crosses the header, the preview cell glows when a setting changes. Never on your real healing frames, and Reduced motion turns it off.

Before release, this window was tested in game line by line, more than a hundred and thirty checks, and its code was reviewed in full: everything that was off is fixed below. This version also brings what was missing day to day: your Lyorune macros now go on your action bars, every player shows their real resource (a warrior's rage is no longer a blue mana bar), and the mana recovered announcement goes out at the right time even when the game hides your mana.

**Nothing to redo**: your settings, clicks, macros and profiles are kept as they are. Take a minute to start the guided tour from the Home page: four short cards present the new window.

Happy healing, everyone. A bug, an idea? Write to me: that is how Lyorune grows.
